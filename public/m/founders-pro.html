The problem and the fix

Why new businesses lose leads

Most new service businesses do not lose customers because of poor work. They lose them because a reply came too late: a missed call, a form left in an inbox overnight, or a quote nobody followed up on.

The problem

Leads slip through while you are busy doing the work

  • Missed calls with no follow-upWhen you are with a client, calls go to voicemail, and many callers never leave a message.
  • Slow replies to website formsA form that waits hours for a response gives the customer time to keep shopping.
  • Forgotten quotesYou meant to check in after a few days. Then the week got busy.
  • Separate vendors for every taskA different company for your filing, website, hosting, and CRM, each with its own invoice.

What a small business CRM setup does for you

A CRM (customer relationship management system) keeps every call, text, form, and booking in one place. Instead of sticky notes and spreadsheets, you see each lead, where it stands, and what happens next.

  • New inquiries are captured and logged automatically
  • Follow-up messages go out on schedule
  • You can see which leads are active and which need attention
  • Customers book online and get confirmations and reminders

What is missed-call text-back?

It is a CRM automation that sends the caller a text message shortly after a call goes unanswered, so the conversation can continue until you are free to reply personally.

Do I need a CRM as a new business?

If customers reach you by phone, web forms, or messages and you rely on follow-up to win work, a CRM helps keep those inquiries from slipping through. Setting it up at launch is easier than moving contacts later.

How long does CRM setup take?

It depends on your services, workflows, and how quickly you share content. We give you an expected timeline after your strategy call.

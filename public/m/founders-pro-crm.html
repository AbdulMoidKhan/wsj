What is a registered agent and why do I need one?

Illinois requires every LLC to name a registered agent who accepts legal notices during business hours. Founders Pro includes registered agent service for one year.

What does done-for-you CRM mean?

Our team builds and configures your CRM for you: pipeline stages, missed-call text-back, booking, follow-up messages, and review requests. You get a working system and an onboarding session.

Is there a separate CRM fee?

No. Your CRM setup and system are included in the Founders Pro price of $325 per month, billed annually ($3,900 per year).

Is the Illinois state filing fee included?

No. The approximate $150 Illinois state filing fee is paid separately to Illinois. The amount may change and is not included in the Workspace Junction package price.

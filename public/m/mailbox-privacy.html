Is this a real address or a PO Box?

It is a real street address with your own mailbox number at our coworking building in Oakbrook Terrace, Illinois. It is not a PO Box.

Can I use the address for my LLC and bank account?

You can list it as your business address. Many banks and platforms reject PO Boxes, and a street address avoids that, but check your bank's specific requirements. The mailbox is not a registered agent service; our Founders Package includes one, so ask our team for details.

Is there a contract?

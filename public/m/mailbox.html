Can I use a mailbox address for my LLC?

You can generally list a commercial street address as your LLC's principal office address. A mailbox is not a registered agent, so you still need a registered agent with an Illinois street address. Confirm current filing requirements with the Illinois Secretary of State.

Can I get my mail 24/7?

Yes. Your mailbox is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at our Oakbrook Terrace building. Our team confirms pickup times for oversized packages when you sign up.

Is a business mailbox the same as a PO Box?

No. A PO Box uses a post office box number. Your Workspace Junction mailbox uses our real street address with your own mailbox number, and we can receive packages from private carriers.

Will my bank accept the address?

Bank policies differ. Many banks reject PO Boxes, and a commercial street address avoids that problem, but some banks have their own rules for mailbox addresses. Ask your bank before you apply.

What do I need to set up my business mailbox?

Choose a plan and complete USPS Form 1583 with two acceptable forms of ID. Federal rules require this form before a commercial mail receiving agency can accept mail for you, and our team walks you through it.
